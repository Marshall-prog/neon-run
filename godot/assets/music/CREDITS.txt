NEON RUN — background music credits
===================================

All 10 background tracks are Creative Commons Zero (CC0 / public domain) and may
be used in personal and commercial projects without attribution. Credit is given
here anyway, because the artists deserve it.

Source corpus: github.com/SoundSafari/CC0-1.0-Music (a mirror of CC0 music from
chosic.com, freepd.com and other public-domain sources).

Every track is instrumental (no vocals). Each one was trimmed to a seamless
~90 s loop and loudness-matched to the same RMS. For the Godot build (2.x) they
were re-encoded as Vorbis OGG (~76-90 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo); the earlier Canvas
build (1.x) shipped the same tracks as Opus (~32 kbps).
